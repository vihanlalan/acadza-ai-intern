How this queue is ordered

Most urgent = most harm to students right now. Score = defect severity × (1 + ln(1 + reports)), plus a bump while a human decision is still pending.

Severity: wrong / malformed key 10 · wrong type 9 · no valid answer 9 · missing data or option 8 · missing figure 7 · duplicate options 7 · wrong solution step 6 · unit not stated 4 · notation / markup 3 · wrong chapter 3 · syllabus 2 · spelling, noise, exam tag 1.

A wrong key marks every correct student wrong; a typo only slows them down. Duplicate reports raise the score, but with diminishing returns, so one loud question can't bury a silent broken key.
